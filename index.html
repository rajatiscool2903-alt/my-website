Our story

Small batches, slow mornings

Maison Kiln & Bloom began as a weekend roasting project in a rented kitchen. Today we roast in small batches, press our juice by hand each morning, and bake everything on site, so the place smells of warm cardamom and bread before the street wakes up.

We keep sourcing simple and traceable, and we’d rather serve fewer things done well than many things done in a hurry.

Before you come in

Can I take my order to go?

Yes. Ask at the counter for takeaway cups or bring your own reusable tumbler.

Is there Wi-Fi and seating for laptops?

Yes, free Wi-Fi and outlets at most tables. Ask for the password at the counter.

Do you have plant-based options?

Oat milk is available for every coffee and tea, and most juices and bakes are plant-based. Ask about the day’s bake.

Can I bring my dog?

Dogs are welcome on the patio. Please keep them outside the indoor seating area.
